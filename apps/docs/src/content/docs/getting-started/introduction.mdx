pushpilot handles the Web Push protocol. You keep authentication, your database, recipient selection, and deployment.

## What the library does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Server">
    Encrypts notifications, signs them with your VAPID keys, and reports which
    subscriptions have expired.
  </Card>
  <Card title="HTTP handler">
    A standard `Request` and `Response` handler calls your `onSubscribe` and
    `onUnsubscribe` callbacks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Browser client">
    Handles permission and subscription state, and reconciles the browser with
    your server.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Service worker">
    Always attempts to show a notification, with a fallback for malformed
    payloads.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## What you keep

pushpilot ships no database adapter, hosted backend, migration, queue, or telemetry. Your application decides who is signed in, where subscriptions live, and who receives each notification.

## Requirements

- The server package requires Node 22 or later, Bun, Deno, or Cloudflare Workers with WebCrypto and fetch.
- React is optional.
- Desktop `localhost` counts as a secure context. A phone needs HTTPS.

See [Runtime and browser support](/guides/platforms/) for platform details, including iOS.
